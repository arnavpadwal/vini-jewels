One batch.
Set by hand.

We make in small batches, not seasons. Every piece is set, enamelled and checked by hand before it goes into the shop — so if something is not right, we catch it before you do.

What goes into a piece

Materials, not shortcuts.

Kundan, meenakari and freshwater pearl, worked on 18k gold-plated brass. We use the traditional techniques properly and say plainly what the piece is made of.

01

Kundan

Stones are set by hand, one at a time, in the traditional kundan technique.

02

Meenakari

Colour is filled into the metal in the meenakari tradition, then finished by hand.

03

Freshwater pearl

Pearl drops and strands are matched by eye, so no two pieces sit exactly alike.

04

18k gold-plated brass

The base is brass, plated in 18k gold and checked piece by piece before it leaves.

Why one batch

Small enough to check by eye.

A batch is a promise that nothing leaves in a hurry. Pieces are matched, checked and finished one by one. When the batch is gone, it is gone — the next one starts from the workbench, not a spreadsheet.
